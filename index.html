About Grape Games

Grape Games is a small, independent team that loves making things in Minecraft. We focus on original, family-friendly content with polished building, clean scripting and a sense of humor.

Every project is made in-house from scratch: no reused or third-party assets, and fully compliant with the Minecraft Usage Guidelines and Marketplace content standards.
