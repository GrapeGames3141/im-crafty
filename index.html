Giant's Abandoned Garden

A giant's garden has been left to grow wild, and you're small enough to get lost in it. Wander between towering mushrooms, climb lookouts in the roots of an ancient tree, find tiny cottages hidden inside a cracked flowerpot, and cross a lily-pad pond beside a rusting garden spade.

  • Five giant mushrooms forming a grove you can walk beneath
  • Three cottages built inside an intact giant flowerpot
  • Two root lookouts on a sprawling ancient tree
  • A pond with lily pads and a crossing
  • A giant pointed spade as a landmark
  • Six dense habitat zones linked by a network of paths
  • TypeCreative world
  • EditionBedrock
  • BuildPure vanilla blocks
  • Versionv1.2.0 candidate

Gold Golem Logistics

Logistics you can watch. Upgrade a copper golem into a Gold Golem, set a request on a Gold Chest, and your golem walks the items over from your supply chests. There's no teleporting and nothing hidden: every delivery is a golem carrying a crate across your base.

  • Gold Chests: 27-slot containers. Put one item in the first slot to request it; leave it empty to make the chest a supplier.
  • Gold Upgrade: turns a copper golem into a Gold Golem that serves chests around its home.
  • Logistics Ledger: sets a golem's range (16–64 blocks) and each chest's request size.
  • Illustrated in-game guide: every player gets the guidebook automatically, with a text view and a picture view.
  • Custom models, textures and animations, including a carry pose when a golem has cargo.
