Giant's Abandoned Garden

TypeWorld (Creative)
PlatformMinecraft: Bedrock Edition
StatusIn development (v1.2.0 candidate)
ContentPure vanilla blocks, no packs or scripts required

Short description

Shrink down and explore a giant's overgrown garden of towering mushrooms, ancient roots and hidden flowerpot cottages.

Long description

A giant once tended this garden. Now it has grown wild, and you're small enough to get lost in it.

Walk beneath a grove of five giant mushrooms, climb lookouts in the roots of an ancient tree, and discover three tiny cottages tucked inside a giant flowerpot. Cross a lily-pad pond, follow winding paths between six lush habitat zones, and find the enormous garden spade the giant left behind.

Built entirely from vanilla blocks, so it works with no extra packs.

Key features

  • Five giant mushrooms forming a walkable grove
  • Three cottages built inside a giant flowerpot
  • Two lookouts in the roots of an ancient tree
  • A lily-pad pond with a crossing
  • A giant pointed spade landmark
  • Six dense habitat zones connected by paths

Gold Golem Logistics

TypeAdd-on (behavior + resource pack)
PlatformMinecraft: Bedrock Edition
StatusIn testing (v0.3.0)
New contentGold Golem, Gold Chest, Gold Upgrade, Logistics Ledger, illustrated guidebook

Short description

Upgrade copper golems into Gold Golems that carry your items between Gold Chests, so you can watch every delivery happen.

Long description

Put your golems to work! Use a Gold Upgrade on a copper golem and it becomes a Gold Golem, a hard-working helper that keeps your chests stocked.

Place one item in the first slot of a Gold Chest to request it, and your Gold Golem walks it over from your supply chests, carrying a crate the whole way. There's no teleporting and nothing hidden, so you can watch every delivery cross your base.

Use the Logistics Ledger to set how far each golem travels and how much each chest asks for. Every player gets an illustrated guidebook to help them get started.

Key features

  • Gold Golems that physically carry items between chests
  • 27-slot Gold Chests that request items with a single sample slot
  • Adjustable golem range (16–64 blocks) and request sizes
  • Illustrated in-game guidebook with text and picture views
  • Custom models, textures and carry animations
